About

Hydrology & GIS Specialist | Flood Risk, Water Resources & Land Use Modelling

I'm a Hydrology and GIS Specialist based in Tanzania, currently working as a freelance Hydrology Technician with Shilungushela firm. My work sits at the intersection of field data collection, advanced spatial analysis, and data visualization for decision-making in water resources and environmental management.

I focus on turning raw spatial and hydrological data into decision-ready outputs: flood risk maps, watershed delineations, runoff models, and land use classifications that support informed policy and development interventions across Africa.
